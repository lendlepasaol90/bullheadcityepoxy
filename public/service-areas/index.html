Our service area

The Colorado River communities of Mohave County, plus Golden Valley and Kingman in the high desert.

Coating work is very sensitive to timing, especially in summer, when the best hours are early in the morning. Keeping our work concentrated along the river corridor lets us arrive while the slab is still cool. Places further out are scheduled with that in mind.

If your town is not listed here, give us a call. We will tell you straight away whether we can help.

  • Bullhead City

    All of the city, from riverfront homes and the Riviera area to neighborhoods along the Parkway and the newer streets climbing toward the foothills. Garages, interiors, patios and commercial floors.

  • Fort Mohave

    Subdivisions and golf community homes south along Highway 95, many with oversized garages and RV bays. Also shops and businesses on the highway.

  • Mohave Valley

    Larger rural lots with detached shops, barns and river properties further south.

  • Golden Shores

    River homes and boat garages near Topock, scheduled by arrangement.

  • Golden Valley

    Homes on acreage and big detached workshops west of Kingman, where winter mornings run much colder than on the river.

  • Kingman

    Homes, businesses and industrial buildings up the hill. Higher elevation means a different working season, which we plan for.

  • Oatman Road area

    Rural properties and outbuildings toward the Black Mountains, by arrangement.

  • Needles, CA

    Commercial enquiries across the state line, by arrangement and subject to the requirements for work in California.

Services available across the area

  • Garage Floor Coatings: Garages, boat bays and RV garages
  • Residential Epoxy Flooring: Living areas, casitas, laundry and game rooms
  • Decorative Flake Flooring: Chip blends that disguise river sand

  • Polyaspartic Floor Coatings: Fast-curing, sun-stable coatings

  • Patio & Concrete Coatings: Patios, pool decks, walks and outdoor kitchens
  • Commercial Epoxy Flooring: Restaurants, retail, clinics and offices
  • Warehouse Floor Coatings: Warehouses, fleet buildings and industrial floors
